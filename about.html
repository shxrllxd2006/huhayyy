Historical Backround of Opol Community College

Out of the dreams and aspirations of the people in the community of Opol, its clamor for the establishment of an institution for higher learning that is accessible and affordable to everybody was turned into a reality.

The merging of the Post Secondary courses, Teacher Education and Bachelor of Science in Business Administration was spearheaded by the secondary school Principal II of Opol National Secondary Technical School (ONSTS) Mrs. Conchita L. Maglasang and the Local Chief Executive of Opol, the Hon. Dixon Q. Yasay paved the way to the opening of what is now known as Opol Community College.

By virtue of the Sangguniang Bayan (SB) Resolution No. 2003-155 enacting Ordinance No. 2003-14, marked the creation of a community college in the municipality of Opol. In consortium with the Bukidnon State College now Bukidnon State University, the ordinance was approved on August 12, 2003.

The final and official operation of the college was made possible upon entering into a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) between the Department of Education, represented by Dr. Sinforosa A. Guiñares, Schools Division Superintendent of Misamis Oriental and the Chairman of the Board of Trustees (BOT) of Opol Community College, the Hon. Mayor Dixon Q. Yasay. The college operated within the school grounds of Opol National Secondary Technical School for a period of five (5) years as stipulated in the agreement.
